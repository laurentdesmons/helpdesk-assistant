# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 PTO days per month worked (21 days/year), starting from date of hire. Part-time employees accrue on a pro-rated basis by scheduled hours.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the following calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31, unless local law requires otherwise (check the country-specific addendum, not included in this sample).

## Requesting time off
Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences. Manager approval is required. Same-day or emergency absences should be reported directly to the manager and logged retroactively.

## Unused PTO at termination
Payout of unused PTO at termination is governed by local jurisdiction law and is not a fixed company-wide rule — this varies by country/state and requires case-by-case lookup, not covered in this sample document.

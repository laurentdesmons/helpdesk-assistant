# Benefits Enrollment Guide

## New hire enrollment
New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health, dental, and vision benefits via the Benefits portal. If no election is made within 30 days, the employee is defaulted to the base health plan only — no dental/vision default.

## Open enrollment
Once yearly, typically a 2-week window in November for coverage effective the following January 1. Changes outside this window require a qualifying life event (marriage, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage).

## Qualifying life events
Must be reported within 30 days of the event via the Benefits portal, with supporting documentation (e.g., marriage certificate, birth certificate). Late reporting forfeits the mid-year change window; the employee must wait for open enrollment.

## Questions on plan specifics
This guide covers process only. Plan-specific coverage questions (deductibles, network providers) should route to the benefits carrier directly, not IT/HR help desk.

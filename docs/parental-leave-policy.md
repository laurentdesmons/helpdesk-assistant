# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed 6 months of continuous employment are eligible for company-paid parental leave, in addition to any statutory leave required by local law.

## Duration
Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid.
Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid.
These figures are illustrative sample values only and do not reflect any real company's actual policy.

## Process
Employee notifies manager and HR at least 30 days before the expected leave start date where possible. HR issues a leave confirmation letter with exact dates and pay continuation details.

## Return to work
Employees may request a phased return (reduced hours for up to 4 weeks) — subject to manager and HR approval, not guaranteed.

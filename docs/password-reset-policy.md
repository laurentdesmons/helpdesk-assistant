# Password Reset Policy

## Self-service reset
Employees can reset their own password via the self-service portal (portal.example-corp.internal/reset) provided they have previously enrolled a recovery method (personal email or authenticator app). Self-service resets do not require IT involvement.

## Assisted reset
If self-service is unavailable (recovery method not enrolled, account locked after failed self-service attempts), the employee's identity must be verified by IT support before a manual reset is issued. Acceptable verification:
- Video call with camera on, employee shows badge, OR
- Manager confirmation via a message sent from the manager's known corporate account.

Manual resets are never issued based on a phone call alone.

## Password requirements
Minimum 12 characters, must include at least one number and one symbol. Passwords cannot be reused from the last 10 passwords. Passwords expire every 180 days.

## Scope
This policy covers corporate domain accounts only. Third-party SaaS application passwords are managed separately by each application owner.
